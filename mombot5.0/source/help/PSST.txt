     Planet SST using two ships and two planets.
        
     Usage:  psst [ship2#] [planet1#] [planet2#]
             psst clear_busts
        
        [ship2#] - empty second ship in the starting sector.
        [planet1#] [planet2#] - movable planets in the starting sector.
        [clear_busts] - clears cached bust data.
        Steals equipment from xxB ports, xports between ships, and refurbishes.
        Run from Command.
