                     
   
                <<<< plimper >>>>
   
     Keeps personal limpets loaded in the current sector.
        
     Usage:  plimper {saveme}
        
        {saveme} - start CK saveme before running.
        Run from Citadel, blue alignment, with T-warp and Stardock known.
        Refurbs limpets at Stardock and reloads the sector after limp hits.
