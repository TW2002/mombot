MOVE - Product Mover

    move [type] [planet|all] [rounds|amount]

    [type] - use [f]uel, [o]rg, [e]quip 
    [type] - use [fc] fuel colo, [oc] org colo, [ec] equip colo
    [planet] planet to move to, or [all] for every planet in sector
    [rounds] number of rounds to move product / colonists
    [amount] with [all], amount of product to move to each planet
